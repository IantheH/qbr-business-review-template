# QBR Business Review Template

A platform-agnostic Customer Business Review and QBR framework for SaaS teams, Customer Success Managers, Technical Account Managers, and account leaders.

This framework helps customer-facing teams connect product adoption, customer goals, business outcomes, risks, recommendations, and next steps in a clear executive-facing format.

---

## Purpose

A strong QBR is not a product demo.

It is a business conversation that helps the customer understand:

- what progress has been made
- how the platform supports their goals
- where adoption is strong
- where risk or friction exists
- what should happen next
- how both teams will stay aligned

The goal is to move the conversation from “what features are being used” to “what business value is being created.”

## QBR Structure

A strong customer business review typically includes:

### 1. Executive Summary

A short overview of what was accomplished, what changed, and what should be prioritized next.

### 2. Customer Goals

A review of the customer’s original goals and whether those goals are still accurate.

### 3. Adoption & Usage

A review of how the customer is using the platform, which workflows are active, and where adoption gaps exist.

### 4. Business Outcomes

A discussion of what value the customer has received and how the platform supports business priorities.

### 5. Risks & Blockers

A review of unresolved issues, adoption risks, stakeholder concerns, or technical blockers.

### 6. Recommendations

A set of practical next steps that support customer goals, adoption, and long-term value.

### 7. Action Plan

Clear ownership, due dates, and follow-up commitments.

---

## Customer Success Principle

A QBR should help the customer see value clearly.

The strongest reviews are not centered on features. They are centered on outcomes, priorities, risks, and the customer’s path forward.
