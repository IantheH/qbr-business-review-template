# QBR Agenda Template

Use this template to structure a Quarterly Business Review or Customer Business Review with a SaaS customer.

The goal is to align stakeholders, review progress, discuss adoption, identify risks, and agree on clear next steps.

## Meeting Goals

The goals of this review are to:

- Review progress against customer goals
- Discuss product adoption and usage trends
- Identify business outcomes and value delivered
- Review risks, blockers, or support concerns
- Align on recommendations and next steps
- Confirm ownership and follow-up actions
